NetworkReconciler.php : 33

The `network` namespace of a §27.6 manifest (CONTRACT.md §27.6.2, contract 1.62): what `plan` reads, and how `apply` writes the three kinds — the tenant's policy, its segments and their reply profiles — through the generated {@see NetworkApi}.

Rules this class keeps, each one the contract's:

  • Reads are GETs, walked to exhaustion (get_policy, list_segments, list_profiles), and every stated member is judged on the value as read: a profile's session_timeout_secs is the effective, clamped one. No hidden or stored value is compared and none is kept.
  • An Update is a full, version-conditional replacement built from the read — the stated members replace the read's, the unstated ones are carried from it, a member stated as none (PHP null in the entity's fields) is left out of the body — sent with the version plan read, exactly. A segment's kind, site_key and name are never in it.
  • A profile carries its segment's version, and when this same apply wrote the segment that is the write's response's, not the one plan read (NetworkApplyState).
  • Nothing is retried, re-read or adjusted. A 409 (a ConflictError with its reason_code) or a 403 is the action's failure, and apply stops there.
