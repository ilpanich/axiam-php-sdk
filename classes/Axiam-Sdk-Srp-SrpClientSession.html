SrpClientSession
in package
  • Application

FinalYes
SrpClientSession.php : 17

One SRP exchange's client half: the ephemeral secret `a` held between the challenge request and the proof that answers it (CONTRACT.md §23.2).

A session is single-use. a is drawn fresh per exchange by self::begin() and there is no way to supply one there, because reusing it across logins leaks the relationship between two session secrets (§23.3 rule 7).
